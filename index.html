About Blocca

Your space to create.

Blocca is a single-user creative experience for Meta Quest. Use your Touch controllers to move around your workspace, build with blocks, and explore your own ideas.

There is no set way to create. Start with a simple shape, experiment with color, and see where your imagination takes you.
